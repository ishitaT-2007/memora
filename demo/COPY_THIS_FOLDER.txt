COPY THIS WHOLE FOLDER
======================

Folder: docs\demo\

Copy the entire "demo" folder (all files inside) to a USB drive or to your
other PC, then place it under your Accrue git repo as:

  <your-repo>\docs\demo\

What you get:
  DEMO_SHEET.html          - image document (layout + static Ask queries)
  DEMO_VIDEO.html          - autoplay 60s demo walkthrough (open in browser)
  accrue-demo-layout.png   - annotated layout image
  README.md                - short index

How to open on any PC (offline, no install):
  1. Double-click DEMO_SHEET.html
  2. Double-click DEMO_VIDEO.html

Do NOT need to commit from the development PC. Add/commit on the system
where your git remote lives, when you are ready.
